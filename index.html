Pioneering the Aerial Frontier of Bangladesh

Blitz Drone Solutions LTD was established with a singular vision: to revolutionize the aerial imaging and survey landscape across Bangladesh. In a fast-paced industrial era where data collection and surveillance have traditionally been slow and labor-intensive, we wanted to build a haven for genuine technology enthusiasts who appreciate the power of industrial unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs).

We source our brand new, high-grade cinematic and surveyor drone units directly from certified international developers, bringing them straight to our custom service facility right here in Dhaka. From customized farming inspections to volumetric mapping, we elevate your workflow to make it seamless, secure, and stress-free.
